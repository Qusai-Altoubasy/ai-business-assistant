# Refund and Return Policy

- Policy ID: POL-REF
- Version: 1.0
- Effective date: 2026-01-01
- Currency: Jordanian Dinar (JOD), amounts with 2 decimal places.
- Scope: This policy sets when and how a customer can return purchased items and how the resulting refund is calculated, issued and timed.

## Return window and return requests

**REF-1** A customer can return an item for a refund only when the return request is made no later than 30 calendar days after the order_date of the order. The same 30-day limit applies to a damaged, defective or wrong item, which is accepted only when the customer reports it to the company no later than 30 calendar days after the order_date of the order and it reaches the company no later than 14 calendar days after the company approves that report.

**REF-2** A return request states the order ID and the products and quantities to be returned. A return can cover all or only some of the items in an order.

**REF-3** After the company approves a return request, the customer returns the items to the company. A returned item is accepted only when it reaches the company no later than 14 calendar days after the company approves the return request; a returned item that reaches the company later is not accepted and is not refunded.

## Condition of returned items

**REF-4** A returned item is accepted for a refund only when it is unused, has no damage caused after the customer received it, is complete with every part, cable, accessory and manual it was sold with, and is returned in its original packaging. Original packaging that has been opened is accepted, except for a product in the Audio category (for example the Headset) whose sealed packaging has been opened and a product in the Stationery category (for example the Notebook) that has been opened or written in, which cannot be returned for a refund. These conditions do not apply to a damaged, defective or wrong item, which is accepted only when the customer reports it to the company no later than 30 calendar days after the order_date of the order and it reaches the company no later than 14 calendar days after the company approves that report.

**REF-5** A returned item that is not unused, has damage caused after the customer received it, is not complete with every part, cable, accessory and manual it was sold with, or is not returned in its original packaging is not accepted, no refund is issued for it, and it remains the customer's property. These conditions do not apply to a damaged, defective or wrong item, which is accepted only when the customer reports it to the company no later than 30 calendar days after the order_date of the order and it reaches the company no later than 14 calendar days after the company approves that report.

## Non-returnable items

**REF-6** An item cannot be returned for a refund when it is a product in the Audio category (for example the Headset) whose sealed packaging has been opened, for hygiene reasons; a product in the Stationery category (for example the Notebook) that has been opened or written in; or any item whose return request is made more than 30 calendar days after the order_date of the order. These conditions do not apply to a damaged, defective or wrong item, which is accepted only when the customer reports it to the company no later than 30 calendar days after the order_date of the order and it reaches the company no later than 14 calendar days after the company approves that report.

**REF-7** A product in the Audio category (for example the Headset) whose sealed packaging has been opened, or a product in the Stationery category (for example the Notebook) that has been opened or written in, can still be returned when it is a damaged, defective or wrong item. Such an item is accepted only when the customer reports it to the company no later than 30 calendar days after the order_date of the order and it reaches the company no later than 14 calendar days after the company approves that report, and the customer then receives a replacement or a refund. A replacement is possible only when the product's stock_quantity is at least the quantity to be replaced; otherwise the customer receives a refund instead of a replacement.

## Refund amount and restocking fee

**REF-8** The refund amount for a returned item is the unit price paid on the order line (order_items.price) multiplied by the quantity returned, in JOD. The refund is never based on the product's current list price.

**REF-9** A restocking fee of 15% of the refund amount is deducted when an item in the Displays category (for example the 27-inch Monitor) is returned with its original packaging opened. No restocking fee is charged for any other item, for a Displays item returned with its packaging still sealed, or for any damaged, defective or wrong item.

## Refund processing time

**REF-10** A refund is issued no later than 5 business days after the returned item is received by the company. Business days are Sunday through Thursday, excluding official public holidays announced by the Government of Jordan; Friday and Saturday are not business days.

## Damaged, defective and wrong items

**REF-11** An item counts as damaged when it was received physically damaged, as defective when it does not work as described on its first use, and as wrong when the product or quantity received differs from the order lines of the order. The customer reports a damaged, defective or wrong item to the company no later than 30 calendar days after the order_date of the order, giving the order ID and a photo or written description of the problem.

**REF-12** A damaged, defective or wrong item is accepted only when the customer reports it to the company no later than 30 calendar days after the order_date of the order and it reaches the company no later than 14 calendar days after the company approves that report. For an accepted item, the customer chooses either a replacement of the same product at no cost or a refund of the unit price paid multiplied by the quantity affected, and no restocking fee is charged. A replacement is possible only when the product's stock_quantity is at least the quantity to be replaced; otherwise the customer receives a refund instead of a replacement.

**REF-13** A replacement for a damaged, defective or wrong item is provided no later than 1 business day after the damaged, defective or wrong item is received by the company. Business days are Sunday through Thursday, excluding official public holidays announced by the Government of Jordan; Friday and Saturday are not business days. A replacement is possible only when the product's stock_quantity is at least the quantity to be replaced; otherwise the customer receives a refund instead of a replacement.

**REF-14** A damaged, defective or wrong item that the customer reports to the company more than 30 calendar days after the order_date of the order receives no replacement or refund.
